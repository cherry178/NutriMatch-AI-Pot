Adaptive Hybrid Weighting Matrix

NutriMatch AI Pot calculates personalized candidate rankings using an ensemble combining SVD Matrix Factorization, Item-Based Collaborative Filtering, and Content-Based TF-IDF. Whenever you submit feedback on a recipe, an Online Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) step adapts your latent coordinates in real time.

Understanding Recommendation Metrics: Ranking metrics (NDCG, Recall, MAP, Hit Rate) measure whether relevant recipes appear at the very top of the user's feed. Rating prediction metrics (RMSE, MAE) measure absolute star-rating error. The Adaptive Hybrid engine maximizes top-k ranking discovery, while pure SVD minimizes rating error.

Optimal Generalization

Validation error reaches global minimum at Epoch 4 (0.5356). Beyond Epoch 4, training loss drops marginally while validation RMSE remains flat, confirming the L2 penalty (λ=0.05) halts generalization degradation.

Zero Overfitting Signature

The spread between Train RMSE (0.5234) and Val RMSE (0.5364) at Epoch 8 is merely 0.013, proving negligible variance gap on held-out reviews.

Regularized Convergence

SGD learning rate = 0.01 with bias regularization (λ=0.05) ensures smooth monotonic loss descent without oscillations.
